# ResonaDeck 1.4.9 | Jellyfin Sign-In, Keep Playing & GTM Sync

**Developer:** Valentine  
**Release type:** Stable public release · Published November 5, 2026  
**Published asset:** `ResonaDeck-1.4.9.zip`

The 1.4.2–1.4.9 update series brings music-source usability, stronger controller navigation, Jellyfin authentication polish and consistent GTM visualizer controls.

## What's new
- **Two-way visualizer state:** ResonaDeck displays GTM's live ON/OFF state when reachable; controls in either plugin can change the same visualizer. Compatible versions need not match.
- **Keep Playing:** selecting a single music track can populate Up Next when Keep Playing is enabled, without changing audiobook/podcast handling.
- **Premium source identities:** dedicated Plex and Jellyfin icons on source cards and switching controls, with clearer Account and Settings navigation.
- **Jellyfin sign-in flow:** Quick Access routes signed-out users to Full Manager; credential editing uses controller-selectable, glass-styled address/username/password controls.
- **Steam keyboard-friendly editing:** a top-positioned, compact active editor keeps the field visible while the native Steam keyboard is displayed. The bottom Nothing Playing banner is hidden during Jellyfin sign-in or reconnect.
- **Controller and glass polishing:** stable directional movement, improved focus contrast, compact developer branding and consistent Account styling.
- **Diagnostics and distribution:** Plex buffer-interruption observations in System Health, and a slimmer plugin ZIP with redundant artwork removed.

## Compatibility and transparency
- Jellyfin editor operation, field visibility and D-pad progression were confirmed on a physical Steam Deck by the maintainer.
- Plex buffering diagnostics do not guarantee all short audio dropouts have been eliminated.
- ResonaDeck runs without GTM. Compatible GTM releases can share visualizer status and control.
- Steam controls its native keyboard appearance.
