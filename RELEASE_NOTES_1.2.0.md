# ResonaDeck 1.2.0 Stable

**Promoted from 1.1.9 TEST after real Steam Deck confirmation.**

ResonaDeck brings your own Plex music, audiobooks, podcasts, and other audio into the Decky Loader Quick Access Menu and Full Manager.

## What's new

- **Surprise Me:** discover and play music across Plex libraries with track/collection choices, themed settings, and Favorites-only options.
- **Keep Playing:** automatic queue replenishment, recent-repeat protection, bounded queue history, and 30-minute/1-hour/2-hour listening modes.
- **Automatic music crossfade:** real dual-stream playback and an equal-power transition. **The eight-second setting was confirmed working audibly on the creator's Steam Deck.** Off, three-, five-, and ten-second settings are also available but haven't received the same hardware validation.
- **Record-to-cover transition:** an upcoming vinyl previews beside the cover and, when the second stream is playing, transitions through the gold turntable to full-square album artwork.
- **Live XFADE indicator:** countdown, buffering, start, fade progress, completion, and blocker information.
- **Personal listening:** Favorites browser, saved sessions, local playlists, artwork prefetch and fallbacks, sleep timer, and controller-friendly A/X actions.

## Stable release policy

1.2.0 is a conservative promotion of the hardware-tested 1.1.9 playback build. The audio engine, artwork handoff, protected idle turntable, Favorites, and controller behavior are intentionally unchanged apart from version identity and documentation.

The creator confirmed the core experience on real Steam Deck hardware. Edge cases across codecs, network conditions, other crossfade durations, alternative themes, and long-form resume are not exhaustively tested.

## Installation

After the new release asset is uploaded, download **ResonaDeck-1.2.0-Stable.zip** from the 1.2.0 GitHub Release and install through **Decky Loader → Settings → Developer → Install Plugin ZIP**. Sign in to a reachable Plex server. Your own Plex media is required.
