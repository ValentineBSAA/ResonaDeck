# 🎧 ResonaDeck 1.5.7 | Quick Menu Performance & Visualizer Integration

**Stable public release · November 6, 2026 · Developer: Valentine**

[Download installable ZIP](https://github.com/ValentineBSAA/ResonaDeck/releases/download/1.5.7/ResonaDeck-1.5.7.zip) · [SHA-256](RELEASE_ASSET_SHA256_1.5.7.txt)

Hey everyone, Valentine here! This update focuses on restoring the look of ResonaDeck's player while cutting down unnecessary Quick Menu work and improving its partnership with Game Theme Music.

## What changed

### ⚡ Quicker, calmer Quick Menu rendering

- Reuse cached theme colors rather than repeatedly sampling large portions of Steam's interface.
- Narrow theme-change observation and reduce unnecessary background polling.
- Improve shutdown cleanup for timers, listeners and the visualizer bridge during plugin hot updates.
- Preserve Steam/CSS Loader dynamic themes and the existing artwork-tinted appearance.

### 🎨 Corrected player styling regression

An intermediate optimization mounted ResonaDeck styling outside the plugin's effective rendering root. This caused oversized artwork, overlapping text and missing or misplaced playback controls. Styles have been restored to the correct scope so the full player uses its intended layout. The maintainer confirmed styling and controls were restored on physical Steam Deck.

### 🌈 GTM 4.6.7 visualizer sharing

- Startup no longer sends a saved ON preference as if the user just pressed ON.
- Visualizers stay hidden on idle Home and may appear during eligible music playback when enabled.
- Deliberate OFF hides visuals without stopping Plex playback.
- Audio analysis and sharing of frequency data to GTM remain supported.

**Known behavior to be aware of:** in the maintainer's test, the scene appeared with music but initially didn't react to audio; toggling **GTM Visualizers** OFF and back ON inside ResonaDeck restored actual music-reactive movement. A universally reliable first-connection handshake on every Steam/Decky session has **not been established**. This manual reconnect is a workaround if the issue recurs.

### 🧹 Cleanup

Removed the Cash App button, embedded logo artwork and donation link. No donations UI is required for playback.

### 🎵 What remains

Plex and Jellyfin music, audiobooks/podcasts, Surprise Me, Favorites, Keep Playing, saved queues, Full Manager, 3/5/8/10-second crossfade, accurate long-duration countdown, three-hour track handling, sleep/wake recovery and audio priority with GTM.

## Installation

Select **ResonaDeck-1.5.7.zip** under the [published release](https://github.com/ValentineBSAA/ResonaDeck/releases/tag/1.5.7). Install through Decky's manual ZIP installer, not GitHub's autogenerated Source code archive. Your previous settings are intended to remain intact.

A full Steam Deck restart is **not normally required**. If an old stylesheet or listener persists after hot updating, close/reopen Quick Access and restart Steam if needed.
